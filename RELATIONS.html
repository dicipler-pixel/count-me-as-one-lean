What the map says

  • One polynomial ties three things. z² − z + 1 is the regular ideal tetrahedron (Thurston), the Riley parameter of the figure-eight knot group, and the monodromy of the trefoil. The amphichiral knot's hyperbolic geometry is built from the chiral knot's monodromy polynomial.
  • The broken branch is a ruler. The golden branch z² − z − 1 also solves Thurston's bare edge equation but fails the cycle test by a sign (−1 instead of +1). Squared, it is exactly the figure-eight monodromy t² − 3t + 1, which is the figure-eight's Alexander polynomial. Kept, not discarded.
  • One ladder. xk + x−k obeys tk+1 = T tk − tk−1 in any ring. T = 1 (trefoil) is periodic with period 6; T = 3 (figure-eight) reaches 123 = φ10 + φ−10 at the fifth rung, Q1's ratio; T = 6 reaches 34, the Apéry / Catalan trace. |T| = 2 is the band edge where the rails merge, and both Riley meridians sit exactly there.
  • D25/D27 join by proof, not analogy. Every trefoil wall on S¹ (Δ3₁(e2πiα) = 0, α ≡ ±1/6) is a root of z² − z + 1, so CMaO's ring-generic Gluing theorems evaluate there: it solves Thurston's edge equation, each tetrahedron gives −1, z³ = −1 (CMaOBridge). D25's symmetrized Alexander polynomials equal CMaO's Seifert-matrix coefficients. The figure-eight has no wall (Δ4₁ ≥ 1). Chiral eta and the BHL q1 separation (2 vs 1) are kept differences.
  • The chiral source survives. The figure-eight has an explicit mirror certificate; the trefoil provably has none (its form is negative definite).
